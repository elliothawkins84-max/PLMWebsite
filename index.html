MATERIALS
OUR WAVELENGTHS
MATERIAL / 01

Metal

High precision permanent marks onto stainless steel, aluminum, titanium, brass, copper, and other metals. Ideal for serialization, traceability, logos, bar codes, part identification, and fine-detail graphics where permanence, legibility, and durability are critical.

MATERIAL / 02

Plastic

Clean, high-contrast/ permanent marks for identification across a broad range of engineered and molded plastics. Ideal for components, electronic, controls, housings, face plates, adapters, and technical parts where conventional labels or printing may not provide the required durability or precision.

MATERIAL / 03

Glass

Laser marking produces a controlled frosted etched effect with exceptional detail and consistency. Ideal for premium branding, laboratory glassware, bottles, architectural applications, and specialty products that require a durable, chemical-resistant mark.

MATERIAL / 04

Organics

Wood, leather, paper, cork, textiles, and other materials can be precisely engraved to produce crisp detail and depth control. Well-suited for premium branding, personalization, decorative products, signage, and custom fabrication.

MATERIAL / 05

Other

WAVELENGTH / CO₂

10,600 nm

The industry standard for non-metallic materials. The CO2 laser source is ideal for processing wood, acrylic, paper, coated metals and organic materials. Typical spot size of approx. 0.2mm

WAVELENGTH / FIBER

1064 nm

The proven standard for precision metal marking. Note* Permanent, high-resolution marking across a wide range of metals where durability and repeatability are essential. Exceptional for serialization, traceability, logos, barcodes, deep engraving, annealing and production identification. Typical spot size of approximately .05mm in diameter

WAVELENGTH / DIODE

455 nm

Efficient, high-resolution processing for select materials. Note* Blue diode lasers provide excellent absorption on many organic materials and select plastics and coatings. Precision and efficiency make them well-suited for detailed engraving, personalization, prototyping, and applications requiring controlled material removal. Typical spot size of approx. .08mm x 0.1mm

WAVELENGTH / UV

355 nm

Ultra-fine marking with excellent thermal control. Note* UV Laser technology produces extremely precise marks while minimizing heat transfer to the surrounding material. This makes it particularly valuable for sensitive plastics, electronics, glass, coatings, and other applications where fine detail, minimal thermal distortion, and premium cosmetic results are paramount. Typical spot size of approx. 0.02mm
