    How we calculate price

    • Coverage How much of the card your design actually fills. A fuller design takes longer to engrave, so it costs a bit more — a lighter, simpler one costs less.
    • Finish Which finish each part of your design uses. Some finishes take more work to produce than others.
    • Quantity How many cards you order at once. A bigger order spreads the setup work across more cards, so the price per card comes down.
