What is laser engraving?

Laser engraving uses a focused laser beam to physically remove or vaporize a thin layer of material from the surface, creating a visible, textured mark with real depth. Because it removes material rather than just marking the surface, the result is permanent — resistant to fading, abrasion, and most solvents.

It's high-contrast and well suited to logos, artwork, and detailed graphics, and it's highly repeatable — so a one-off custom piece and a large production batch are both handled with the same accuracy.

Questions?

What is laser engraving?

Laser engraving uses a focused laser beam to physically remove or vaporize a thin layer of material from the surface, creating a visible, textured mark with real depth. It's permanent, high-contrast, and works well for logos, artwork, and detailed graphics.

What materials can be laser engraved?

Metal (stainless steel, aluminum, brass, titanium, copper), wood, glass, acrylic, leather, and most engineered plastics can all be laser engraved. The right settings vary by material, which is part of why the process is called precision engraving.

Can laser engraving be done in large quantities?

Yes. Laser engraving is highly repeatable, so runs of one-off custom pieces and large production batches are both handled with the same accuracy and consistency.
